# RosterIQ — AI-Powered Predictive Rostering for Hospitality

## What is RosterIQ?

RosterIQ brings machine learning to hospitality scheduling, helping venue operators create optimized rosters that balance staffing needs, labour costs, and award compliance. Built specifically for Australian hospitality, RosterIQ learns from your historical data, real-time demand signals, and business constraints to recommend the best roster every week.

## How It Works

1. **Connect Your Data**: Link your Tanda account. RosterIQ syncs your employees, shifts, timesheets, and historical schedules.

2. **Add Demand Signals**: Connect foot traffic data (SwiftPOS), event calendars, and weather forecasts for more accurate predictions.

3. **Set Your Rules**: Define minimum/maximum staff, preferred shifts, and your labour cost targets.

4. **Get Recommendations**: Every week, RosterIQ suggests an optimized roster. Review, adjust, and publish directly to Tanda.

5. **Learn & Improve**: The system learns from what you accept/reject to improve future recommendations.
